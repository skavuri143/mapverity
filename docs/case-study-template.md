# Case study template

For teams that want to document their use of MapVerity. Write only what you observed first-hand,
share nothing your organization considers confidential, and get permission before naming your
organization.

1. **Context:** sector, pipeline shape (sources → transformation → target), volume, how it was tested before.
2. **Why MapVerity:** what problem you were trying to solve, and what else you considered.
3. **What you deployed:** version, features used, environment (pilot / pre-production / production), since when.
4. **What happened:** defects found, time saved, coverage or mutation scores. For every number,
   say how you measured it and what you compared it against (a baseline period, the previous
   process). "We don't know yet" is a valid answer.
5. **Limitations:** what did not work, what you had to change, what is still manual.
6. **Contact:** who can confirm these facts (optional).
